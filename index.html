Visual Diary

Tanishq Pandey — Moments, Light & Frames

Photography for me is a way of seeing — slowing down, framing geometric lines, and capturing subtle moods in light and shadow.

This site is my personal visual notebook collecting favorite frames across cities, travels, and everyday walks. From the quiet textures of architectural shadows to golden hour reflections, every frame is an exploration of perspective.
